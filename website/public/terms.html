What Nukkad Kart is

Nukkad Kart lists registered kirana stores and moving sellers (thelas) within 500 m of your address. Orders are between you and the store or seller.

Payment

You pay the store or seller directly at delivery or pickup, by cash or their UPI. Nukkad Kart does not collect payment and gives no refunds; any refund comes from the store. A ₹20 delivery fee below ₹199 is charged by the store.

Orders and cancelling

A store has 5 minutes to accept, otherwise the order is cancelled. You can cancel while the order is Placed or Accepted. Cancelling after the store accepted, or not taking an order, counts towards the limit: 3 in 90 days blocks ordering on your account and number.

Moving sellers (thelas) and location

While a seller has turned on “Live” in the Partner app, customers within 500 m see where the thela is right now on a map. When the seller turns Live off, or moves outside a customer’s 500 m, the location is no longer shown to that customer. Customers must not use a seller’s location for anything other than buying from them.

Call to my street and chat

You can ask a live thela within 500 m to come to your address, one thela at a time. The seller has 3 minutes to answer. You buy and pay the seller in person. If sellers report 3 times in 30 days that they came and nobody was there, calling thelas is paused for 7 days. Chat is text only, phone numbers cannot be sent, and messages are deleted after 24 hours. Either side can block or report.

Delivery code and missing items

When your order is packed you get a 4-digit delivery code. Give it only after you receive your items. If the store does not have an item, it is removed and your total goes down; you can cancel until the order is packed.

Seller obligations

Stores must upload a real photo of their own shop taken in the app at the shop’s location. A false photo or details can lead to suspension. Sellers set their own prices, which must not exceed MRP.

Complaints

Report a problem from the order screen. We pass it to the store or seller, keep a record and may suspend those at fault. Our Grievance Officer (support@nukkadkart.in) acknowledges within 48 hours and resolves within one month.
