1. PURPOSE AND NATURE OF AGREEMENT

1.1 Nukad Kart operates a technology-based hyperlocal marketplace connecting customers with independently operated local stores and vendors.

1.2 Nukad Kart provides a digital platform through which Vendors may display products, receive customer orders, and communicate with customers.

1.3 Nukad Kart does not purchase, own, stock, warehouse, or independently sell the Vendor's listed products.

1.4 The Vendor operates as an independent seller and is responsible for its products, pricing, stock, customer orders, invoicing, and fulfilment.

1.5 This Agreement does not create an employment relationship, agency, franchise, joint venture, or legal partnership between the Parties.

2. VENDOR ELIGIBILITY AND REGISTRATION

2.1 The Vendor must provide accurate and complete business registration details.

2.2 The Vendor shall hold GST registration where it is required by law (for example, when its turnover crosses the applicable threshold) and shall provide its GSTIN in the app. A Vendor that is not required to register may join without a GSTIN.

2.3 The Vendor must possess all licences and registrations legally required for its business activities, including applicable FSSAI registration or licence for food products.

2.4 Nukad Kart may verify submitted business documents and reject incomplete, fraudulent, or non-compliant applications.

2.5 The Vendor shall promptly update any changes to its business information, licences, bank details, or contact information.

3. PRODUCT LISTING AND CATALOGUE MANAGEMENT

3.1 Nukad Kart does not maintain a platform-owned master product catalogue.

3.2 Vendors are responsible for creating and managing their own product listings.

3.3 Vendors may:

- Retrieve eligible product information and images from Open Food Facts (OFF), subject to applicable licence conditions.
- Capture original product photographs.
- Scan product barcodes to obtain available product information.
- Manually enter product names, prices, descriptions, and stock details.

3.4 The Vendor shall verify all product information before publishing any listing.

3.5 The Vendor shall ensure that product images and descriptions are accurate and do not infringe intellectual property rights.

3.6 Open Food Facts information may be incomplete or inaccurate. The Vendor remains responsible for verifying product information, while Nukad Kart shall comply with any applicable obligations arising from its own use of third-party data.

3.7 The Vendor grants Nukad Kart a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to host, display, and technically process authorised listing content for operating and promoting the marketplace during the listing's availability, subject to applicable third-party licences.

3.8 The Vendor must not list counterfeit, expired, prohibited, unsafe, or otherwise unlawful products.

4. PRODUCT PRICING AND INVENTORY

4.1 The Vendor independently determines product selling prices, subject to applicable laws, including MRP restrictions.

4.2 The Vendor shall maintain accurate stock availability and product information.

4.3 The Vendor must not knowingly accept orders for unavailable products.

4.4 Applicable product taxes, packaging declarations, and other statutory requirements shall be the Vendor's responsibility as seller.

4.5 Any applicable discount must be accurately reflected in the customer-facing order value.

5. ORDER PLACEMENT AND ACCEPTANCE

5.1 Customers may discover Vendors and place orders through Nukad Kart.

5.2 The Vendor shall review and accept or reject orders according to the applicable order-management process.

5.3 Upon accepting an order, the Vendor shall make reasonable efforts to fulfil it within the communicated timeframe.

5.4 The Vendor shall promptly inform customers about stock shortages, delays, substitutions, or cancellations.

5.5 Product substitutions require customer consent where applicable.

5.6 Nukad Kart does not guarantee any minimum number of orders, customers, sales, or revenue.

5.7 If the Vendor does not respond to 5 (five) orders in one day, the store is taken offline for the rest of that day. The Vendor may go live again from the Partner App. The store is also hidden outside its declared opening hours.

6. DIRECT PAYMENT POLICY

6.1 Customers shall pay the Vendor directly through the payment methods made available by the Vendor.

6.2 Nukad Kart shall not collect or hold customer product-sale payments on behalf of the Vendor.

6.3 Nukad Kart shall not operate an order-payment settlement system between customers and Vendors under this business model.

6.4 The Vendor shall be responsible for receiving, reconciling, and recording customer payments.

6.5 Nukad Kart shall not charge a commission on product sales.

6.6 Nukad Kart shall not charge customers a platform fee on orders.

6.7 The Vendor shall remain responsible for applicable payment-related compliance, taxes, refunds, and payment disputes.

7. DELIVERY AND FULFILMENT POLICY

7.1 Orders of ₹199 or Above

The Vendor agrees to provide free delivery to customers for eligible orders having a final product subtotal of ₹199 or above.

7.2 Orders Below ₹199

For orders having a final product subtotal below ₹199, the Vendor may collect the applicable delivery charge directly from the customer.

7.3 Ownership of Delivery Charges

The Vendor shall retain 100% of all applicable delivery charges collected from customers.

7.4 Nukad Kart shall not deduct any commission or platform fee from delivery charges.

7.5 The Vendor shall arrange and manage order delivery through its own delivery person, at its own responsibility and expense, subject to applicable laws.

7.6 Delivery charges, if applicable, must be disclosed to the customer before order confirmation.

7.7 The free-delivery threshold shall be calculated using the final product subtotal after product discounts and excluding delivery charges.

7.8 The Vendor shall be responsible for delivery delays, missing items, incorrect deliveries, and damage attributable to its fulfilment operations, subject to applicable consumer rights.

8. VENDOR SUBSCRIPTION PASSES

Nukad Kart operates on a subscription-based revenue model. Current passes:

- Free Trial: ₹0, 7 days (once per Vendor, starts on registration)
- Daily Pass: ₹99, 1 day
- Weekly Pass: ₹199, 7 days
- Monthly Pass: ₹499, 30 days
- Yearly Pass: ₹5,999, 365 days

8.1 New Vendors receive a seven-day free trial, starting on the date of registration. The trial is given once per Vendor.

8.1A A new pass purchased while a pass is active starts when the current pass ends. While no pass or trial is active, the Vendor's store is hidden from customers.

8.2 After expiry of the trial period, the Vendor must purchase a valid subscription pass to continue using subscription-restricted marketplace services.

8.3 Subscription access shall remain active for the purchased validity period, unless suspended or terminated in accordance with this Agreement.

8.4 Subscription payments shall be made to Nukad Kart through its authorised subscription payment facility.

8.5 Any applicable taxes shall be disclosed during subscription checkout.

8.6 Subscription renewal shall not be automatic unless the Vendor separately authorises an automatic renewal arrangement.

8.7 Cancellation, refunds, and early termination shall be governed by the disclosed Subscription Cancellation and Refund Policy, subject to applicable law.

8.8 Nukad Kart may revise subscription prices prospectively after giving appropriate notice. Price changes shall not retrospectively alter an already purchased pass.
